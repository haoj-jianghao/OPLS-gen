## Archive reuse and the context strictness

Seven archive entries fitted on the first batch's fragments were reused by
new molecules:
- `C-OS-CT-CA` (benzyl salicylate);
- `CA-CA-C_2-O_2` from benzaldehyde (ethylvanillin, piperonal);
- terpene alkene keys (α-terpineol, citronellal, isophorone).

Several misses come from the context SMARTS being exact, not from the class
quartet being absent:
- benzaldehyde's `CA-CA-C_2-O_2` (aldehyde C has one H) does not match the
  aryl ketone in acetophenone/haloperidol;
- propylene carbonate's ring `OS-C-OS` does not match diethyl carbonate;
- carvone's cyclohexenone keys do not match isophorone's substitution.

Covering these needs either more fragments in the fit, or a decision to make
the context coarser for some keys.
