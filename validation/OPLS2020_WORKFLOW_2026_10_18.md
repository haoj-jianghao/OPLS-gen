# OPLS/2020 workflow: 100-molecule validation (2026-10-18)

The workflow now uses the OPLS/2020 tables, GAFF2 LJ for atoms OPLS cannot type, and on-the-fly QM fitting of bonded terms missing from the tables. This report covers what changed, the run over the 100 test molecules, and the measured and estimated QM cost. Method details are in the README.

## What changed (2026-10-18)

The goal is no longer to reproduce LigParGen's output. It is now a general OPLS parameterization workflow:

| Stage | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Charges | 1.14*CM1A-LBCC (AMSOL) | unchanged |
| Atom types | Foyer + archive (GROMACS numbering) | same SMARTS, refined to OPLS/2020 type numbers |
| LJ, classes | Foyer XML (GROMACS `ffnonbonded.itp`) | OPLS/2020 `.par` table |
| Bonds, angles, torsions | GROMACS `ffbonded.itp` + archive / boss-like fallbacks | OPLS/2020 `.sb`/`.par` (BOSS lookup rules) |
| Atoms with no OPLS type | error, or curated borrow rules | curated borrow rules, then GAFF2 type + LJ (antechamber) |
| Bonded terms missing from the table | error, or alias/zero fallbacks, or archive | fitted per molecule to QM (`--qm xtb` default, `--qm dft`) |
| Topology | includes GROMACS `oplsaa.ff/forcefield.itp` | self-contained (own `[ defaults ]`) |

The legacy path is still available as `--parameter-set gromacs`; its 52 regression tests still pass.

### Typing changes made during this run

These are all entries in `src/opls_ff_gen/data/atomtype_archive.json`, dated 2026-10-18. Foyer had no SMARTS for these OPLS/2020 types, which first sent 7 atoms to GAFF2.

- `opls2020_types` (20 rules): OPLS/2020 alkane, alkyl-arene, cyclobutane, vinyl/ethynyl-cyclopropane and alkyne-adjacent types.
- New SMARTS:
  - 905 (N-CH3 on a tertiary amine; diphenhydramine);
  - 915 (CH on a tertiary amine; hydroxyzine);
  - 914 broadened (CH on a secondary amine; prilocaine);
  - 244 (secondary-amide N-CH2R; lauramide MEA, which now needs no QM at all);
  - 518 enol/vinyl-ether C (warfarin; generalizes Foyer's own 518);
  - 628/632 purine N-H (theophylline N7-H was typed as an amine).
- Borrow rules `ring-N-CH` and `ring-N-C`: sp3 C on an aromatic ring N (clotrimazole trityl C).
- Precedence for the two Foyer ambiguities in the 10-18 batch:
  - chlorzoxazone C=O is 235 over 567, its ring O is ester 467 over furan 566, and the ring-fusion C is 147 over 567;
  - theophylline C5 is 147 over 917.
- Bug fix: precedence entries for the same type are now merged, not overwritten.

### QM fit quality (GFN2-xTB)

There are 113 scans, 42 of them ring scans. RMSE is QM vs MM relaxed at the same scan geometries, in kJ/mol:

| | Mean | Median | Max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Missing terms zeroed | 8.9 | 7.1 | 50 |
| Fitted | **2.5** | **1.5** | 15.6 |

- In 31 of the 48 molecules every scan fits within 4 kJ/mol.
- 22 scans stay above 4 kJ/mol and are listed as warnings in their ITP. The worst are lauramine oxide CH2-N(O) (15.6), metronidazole C-NO2 (15.2), carbamazepine C(ar)-N(urea) (11.0), lidocaine N-CH2 (10.4) and haloperidol (10.4). In all of them the MM baseline differs from QM by more than a Fourier series on the missing quartets can absorb: strong 1-4 electrostatics, intramolecular hydrogen bonds or steric coupling with a neighbouring group. Better fits would need fragmenting or refitting 1-4/charge terms; that is out of scope for torsions.
- 5 scans end slightly worse than with the term zeroed (all had a zero-model RMSE under 2.2 kJ/mol). Their key is shared with other scans, and the joint fit trades a little accuracy on them for a large gain elsewhere.

Two changes to the fit came out of this run:
- **H-rotor locking:** during MM relaxation, OH/NH2/CH3 rotors are now held at their QM values. Before, a free OH could swing into an H-bond the QM scan did not have. Metronidazole CH2-OH improved from 10.9 to 4.3 kJ/mol.
- **Damped iteration:** the self-consistent iteration now backtracks (step 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8), so the relaxed objective never increases. Before, metronidazole's objective oscillated between 3,000 and 7,500.

Bonds and angles from the modified Seminario method, checked on DEET (xTB) against OPLS/2020 table terms:
- equilibrium values agree to 0.0006 nm and 0.9 degrees on average;
- force constants: median ratio 0.89 for bonds and 0.98 for angles, with about ±30% scatter.

### Estimated for the 100 molecules

From `src/scripts/estimate_qm_cost.py benchmarks/opls2020_xtb_2026_10_18 validation/opls2020/qm_timings.json`.

Method:
- the measured xTB batch is the baseline;
- the xTB-specific steps are replaced by DFT costs from power-law fits to measured timings:
  - single point ~ N^2.1 (def2-SVP) and N^2.6 (def2-TZVP);
  - gradient ~ N^2.5;
  - density-fitted Hessian ~ N^2.7;
- each molecule's actual scan points are used;
- the DFT geometry optimization and Hessian are counted only where bonds or angles are fitted.

The model reproduces the two DFT test molecules that are in the batch: coumarin 360 s estimated vs 364 s measured, and metronidazole 612 s vs 634 s.

| Level | 100 molecules | Largest per molecule |
|---|---:|---|
| GFN2-xTB (measured) | **0.33 h** | 1.6 min |
| B3LYP-D3(BJ)/def2-SVP single points + Hessian | **~12.4 h** | lauramine oxide, warfarin ~1.2 h |
| def2-TZVP single points (SVP Hessian) | **~23.5 h** | warfarin, hydroxyzine ~2.4 h |

- DFT is about 40x the cost of xTB (70x at TZVP). Most of the cost is scan single points (24 per rotatable bond, 13-25 per ring bond). For molecules with fitted bonds/angles, the DFT Hessian adds about 3 min at 20 atoms and 25-45 min at 40-50 atoms.
- Cheaper DFT options, not implemented:
  - xTB Hessian with DFT torsions: saves about 30% overall, since force constants depend less on the method than torsion profiles do;
  - fragmenting around each scanned bond (about 15 atoms instead of 30-50): a 2-5x saving per point on the larger molecules.

## Open items

1. Torsion scans that keep RMSE > 10 kJ/mol (5 molecules, listed above): consider fragment-based scans, or reporting them as needing review.
2. Only one atom environment remains with no OPLS type: the 1,3,4-thiadiazole N (GAFF2 `nd`; `nb` with the built-in GAFF2 table, same LJ).
3. Tertiary amides use Foyer's 235/236/239 (class N). OPLS/2020 lists newer tertiary-amide types 1035-1045 (class NM, "NEW TERT AMIDE PARAMETERS") that the workflow does not use yet.
4. Impropers are applied to every planar centre of the classes that have one (as before). OPLS/2020 itself only lists `O-C-X-Y`, `Z-N-X-Y`, `Z-CA-X-Y` and `Z-CM-X-Y`; nitro N uses `Z-N-X-Y`.
5. DFT mode: optionally use the xTB Hessian, and consider fragmenting for large molecules.
